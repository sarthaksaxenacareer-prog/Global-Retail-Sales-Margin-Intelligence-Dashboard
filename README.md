# 📊 Global Retail Sales & Margin Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end Excel dashboard designed to analyze multi-region retail transaction data, evaluate product category profitability, and track gross margins across online and offline sales channels to drive data-backed strategic pricing and inventory decisions.

## 🛠️ Key Features & Interactive Visuals

* **Executive KPI Cards:** Real-time tracking of Total Revenue, Total Gross Profit, Gross Profit Margin %, Total Units Sold, Total Orders, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
* **Trend Analysis:** Line chart analyzing Monthly Revenue and Gross Profit progression throughout the year.
* **Country Performance:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting the Top 5 Performing Countries by Revenue.
* **Breakdown Analytics:** Donut charts illustrating Channel-Wise and Region-Wise Revenue distributions.
* **Geographic Mapping:** Dynamic Country-Wise Revenue Map visualizer.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Instant data filtering by **Product Category**, **Country**, and **Quarter**.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Top Performing Region:** Central Europe generated the highest total revenue share.
* **Core Sales Channel:** Online Store and Marketplace represent the majority of retail distribution.
* **Margin Stability:** Maintained a stable Gross Profit Margin across fluctuating monthly sales volumes.
